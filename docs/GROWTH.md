# Zero-cash launch and demand validation

**Goal:** get one real organic external visitor, one completed sample quote, one qualified customer conversation and eventually one verified payment — without buying traffic or subscriptions. None are claimed as achieved.

## Before publishing

- Inspect the whole repository for secrets and private freight rates; use only fictitious sample data.
- Test the actual Cloudflare URL and print PDF.
- Confirm Japanese/Chinese/English descriptions reflect current functionality.
- Avoid implying real-time carrier rates, brokerage, customs clearance or legally binding quotation.
- Publish a stable README with actual demo URL, screenshots, installation steps, cost/privacy statement and license.

## Free discovery loop (human review before posting)

1. Technical discoverability: GitHub `README` screenshot + demo link + 5-6 relevant topics. Keep a changelog on real updates, no artificial activity.
2. Search: Japanese queries like `国際物流 見積書 作成 無料`, `海上運賃 見積 テンプレート`; Chinese `国际货代报价单 免费` and English `freight forwarding quote generator free`. Use helpful landing-page content, not duplicated keyword farms.
3. Distribution: publish one genuine product walkthrough on Qiita or Zenn (respecting their promotional restrictions), one professional problem-solving post on LinkedIn, then relevant logistics communities only where sharing is permitted. All posts must be approved and published by an authorized human/account workflow.
4. Customer validation: invite a small number of known freight-forwarding contacts individually, without scraping personal data or mass unsolicited DMs. Ask for honest feedback; avoid paid ads.
5. Measurement: after privacy disclosure and optional free analytics integration, review real referral and usage metrics. Do not use fake clicks or traffic bots. No user-level quote amounts should be logged.

## A simple initial conversion funnel

`Organic discovery → open product → create quote → print PDF → request advanced template → merchant setup and legal review → first real sale`

No payment buttons are enabled in V1. A planned ¥980 premium template is only a **market hypothesis**, not an active product. Merchant verification and legally required disclosure must precede opening paid transactions.
